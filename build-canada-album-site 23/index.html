Call for Canadian musicians

Build Canada: The Album (v2)

Help build
version two.

Musicians across Canada: we’d love to hear what you do with these songs.

Build Canada: The Album began as an experiment. I wrote the lyrics based on memos written by some of Canada’s greatest modern builders, then used AI tools to create the music and produce the first versions of the songs. That first album is a draft—a starting point.

From an AI draft to a Canadian-made record.

After AI-generating the album, we recorded the first song with Canadian musicians: Chris Hadfield (vocals, guitar), Noel Webb (drums, overall direction on the arrangement), Dan Debow (bass, lead guitar), Mike Serbinis (guitar, backup vocals), Sonia Sennik (keyboard, backup vocals), myself (guitar, backup vocals), and Felipe Sena (sound engineer).

Now we want to make Build Canada: The Album (v2) entirely with Canadian musicians.

We’re inviting musicians, singers, bands, producers, and ensembles from every part of Canada to record their own versions of the songs.

You can cover a song as it appears on the original album, or take it somewhere completely new. Change the arrangement. Change the instrumentation. Change the tempo, genre, mood, or feel. Make it folk, rock, hip-hop, country, electronic, punk, jazz, classical—or something we haven’t imagined.

You may also write your own song inspired by one of the other Build Canada memos ↗.

The goal is not to reproduce the AI-generated recordings. It is to see what happens when Canadian artists make these songs their own.

We may include more than one interpretation of the same song on the new album.

Build Canada: The Album (v2) will contain human-only performances and recordings of the songs.

We’ll choose which submissions are included and inform artists by December 15, 2026. No feedback on selection decisions will be provided.

Open-source music

Let the songs travel, change and inspire new versions.

The album will be released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0) ↗.

That means anyone may copy, share, perform, remix, adapt, and build upon the work—including for commercial purposes—provided they give appropriate attribution to the artists.

If you’re a musician anywhere in Canada—from a basement studio to a conservatory, from a small-town band to an established recording artist—we invite you to be part of it.

Pick a song. Make it yours. Record it. Send it in by November 30. Let’s build the next version of the album—and Canada—together.
